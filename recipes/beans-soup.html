Beans Soup (Miyan Wake)

Miyan Wake is a popular Northern Nigerian delicacy. Miyan Wake is simply Beans soup, similar to the popular Yoruba Gbegiri.

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Black Eyed OR Brown Beans
  • 2 Cups Chicken or Beef Stock (Optional)
  • 1 Cooking Spoon Palm oil
  • 1 Medium Smoked Fish Shredded (Optional)
  • Cooked Assorted Meats (Optional)
  • 1 Bell Pepper
  • 1 Scotch Bonnet
  • 1 Small Onion
  • 1/2 Inch Ginger Root
  • 3 Garlic Cloves
  • Small chunk of Dawadawa
  • 2 Bouillon cubes
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Blend the Peppers, Ginger, Garlic, Onion, Dawadawa together till smooth, then set aside. Pick and peel dehull your beans, then cook on high heat till soft.
  2. When it’s soft, mash it up till smooth or leave to cool, then pour in your blender and blend till smooth. You need a semi fluid consistency so add enough water to dilute. If you’re using stock, add this as well, then place on a hob on medium heat and pour in the Palm Oil.
  3. Stir and make sure it’s well combined, then add the Blended Pepper, Onion, Garlic, Ginger and Dadawa mix, you’ll need 2 cooking spoonfuls. Also add the Bouillon cubes and Salt to taste.
  4. Leave to cook for 6-8 minutes, then add the cooked meats. Leave to cook for 5 minutes, then add the shredded fish if you’re using any
  5. Leave to cook for 5 minutes and it’s ready…
  6. Serve and enjoy!
